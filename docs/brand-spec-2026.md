## 1. Brand Essence & Positioning

### Mission
To provide the UK poultry agriculture industry with fully compliant, ethically trained, and reliably transported harvesting teams — while guaranteeing agricultural operatives dignified employment, above-market weekly pay, and premium door-to-door welfare.

### Core Truths
1. **Zero Compromise Welfare**: Full adherence to Lantra Level 2 Humane Handling Standards (two-leg lift with breast support, zero-drop tolerance).
2. **Door-to-Door Guarantee**: Operatives never wait at cold bus stops; heated company minibuses pick up from home addresses and return home after shifts.
3. **Punctual BACS Payroll**: Direct weekly Friday payments with transparent itemized digital payslips; zero deductions for PPE, training, or transport.
4. **GLAA Regulated Integrity**: Operating strictly under Gangmasters and Labour Abuse Authority licence `PULL0001`.

## 3. Typography Architecture

- **Primary Display & Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 600, 700, 800)
  - Clean geometric humanist sans-serif with high x-height for instantaneous readability on mobile and dashboard surfaces.
- **Editorial Sub-headings & Pull Quotes**: `Instrument Serif` (Weights: 400, 400i)
  - Adds refined agricultural craftsmanship and prestige editorial balance to industrial clarity.
- **Technical & Logistics Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 500, 700)
  - Used for hourly/weekly pay rates, GLAA licence IDs, transit route codes, shift timings, and status tags.

## 4. Logo Anatomy & Wordmark Rules

The canonical brand mark consists of two locked components:
1. **The Chicken-C Monogram** (`viewBox="0 0 416 394"`):
   - Circular "C" silhouette forming the breast and tail contour.
   - Stylized 3-point comb head, proud beak, and wattle.
   - Precise circular eye aperture (`cx="253.6" cy="125.4" r="12.4"`).
2. **The Wordmark Lockup**:
   - The monogram serves as the initial "C", docked with negative tracking (`-mr-1` / `-4px`) into the bold geometric text **`atchingJobs.`**
   - The trailing period is set in Cadmium Yellow (`#FFCC00`).
